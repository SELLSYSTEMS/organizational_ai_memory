## Canonical Judge Sequence

1. Open `/capabilities` and run `Prepare a website delivery handoff`.
2. Show that three stored assets and their provenance are selected before any
   generated adaptation.
3. Show the one-human/one-agent onboarding and succeeded physical provisioning.
4. Capture an owner-only experience and demonstrate the correct negative result.
5. Share the current revision and show ID/revision/hash citation.
6. Edit it, demonstrate automatic access reset, then explicitly re-consent.
7. End with the physical runtime proof: protected reads and direct PostgreSQL
   are denied while brokered cited retrieval is allowed.

The submission should avoid the terms "digital twin," "employee clone," and
"company brain." They imply identity substitution or autonomous authority that
the product deliberately does not grant.
