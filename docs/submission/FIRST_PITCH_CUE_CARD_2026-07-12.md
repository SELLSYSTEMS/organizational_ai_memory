# First Pitch Cue Card

Round 1 target: `2:45` inside `3:00`, followed by `1:00` Q&A.

Before presenting, replace `[YOUR ROLE]` with one truthful phrase such as
`Founder`, `AI Architect`, or `Project Lead`.

## Opening - Slide 1 - `0:00-0:20`

> I am Iurii Paimurzin, [YOUR ROLE] at SELLSYSTEMS. We built Organizational AI
> Memory for P5 so a delivery lead starts with approved prior work. Companies
> keep the final document but lose the prompt, workflow, decision context, and
> reason it worked. We turn that know-how into reviewed, versioned, cited
> capability that authorized people and agents can reuse at task start.

## Problem - Slide 2 - `0:20-0:50`

> The failure is not storage. A prompt folder cannot prove what is current, why
> it worked, who may reuse it, or what changed after review. Enterprises also
> cannot expose private employee memory to every agent. We built for authority
> and context: deterministic retrieval, source lineage, private-by-default
> experience, revision consent, independent review, and fail-closed access.

## Workflow - Slides 3 and 4 - `0:50-1:20`

> A delivery lead starts with a task, not a chatbot. PostgreSQL selects an
> approved prompt, workflow, and decision before any model runs. Codex Spark may
> adapt only those records, and every citation is validated. Personal experience
> stays owner-only until consent for the current revision; a distinct locked
> agent retrieves it only through a broker. The model can adapt the plan, but it
> cannot decide what the company knows.

## Evidence - Slide 5 - `1:20-1:40`

> The live release passed 275 API and security tests, 22 build-bound identity
> controls, 35 browser checks, and seven physical-agent boundary checks. A
> delivery lead gets an approved cited starting point instead of rebuilding from
> scratch. A bounded pilot will measure reuse, recommendation acceptance, review
> turnaround, and denial success. We do not claim ROI yet.

## One-Minute Q&A

- **Why not a vector database?** Retrieval similarity cannot establish current,
  reviewed organizational truth. Our records add version, provenance, review,
  and enforceable scope.
- **What does the model control?** Bounded wording only. PostgreSQL selects the
  records; citations are validated; deterministic fallback remains available.
- **Is it production-ready?** It is a live enforced MVP. Enterprise SSO/SCIM,
  distributed sessions, controlled connectors, and outcome analytics are the
  explicit next deployment steps.
- **Where is business value?** It reduces repeated reinvention and time finding
  approved prior work. A bounded pilot will measure that; we do not invent ROI.
- **Is the agent a digital twin?** No. It is a separate principal limited to
  explicitly consented current revisions.
- **What happens when an employee leaves?** Workspace, pair, broker authority,
  and runtime are revoked; separately reviewed organizational capability remains.

Answer format: conclusion first, one proof, then user value. Stop after 20-25
seconds so the judge can ask the next question.

## Delivery Rules

1. Speak the first two sentences 15 percent slower than feels natural.
2. Look at one judge for one sentence, then move to another.
3. Pause after: "The failure is not storage."
4. Do not apologize, narrate every UI control, or add architecture details.
5. If time is called, deliver the closing sentence immediately.
